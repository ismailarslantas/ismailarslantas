### 🦁 Lionstone Pro & Studio — now free

<a href="https://github.com/ismailarslantas/lionstone"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ismailarslantas/lionstone/main/assets/pro-studio/01-hero.jpg" alt="Lionstone Pro & Studio are now free — open source, Apache-2.0, local-first, no license keys, no watermark" width="100%"></a>

I spent two decades cutting podcasts, interviews and talks by hand. **[Lionstone Pro & Studio](https://github.com/ismailarslantas/lionstone)** turns that craft into an AI video editor that runs on your own computer — and every feature is now **free and open source** (Apache-2.0). No subscription, no license key, no activation, no watermark, no uploads.

| Release | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **[v0.3.1](https://github.com/ismailarslantas/lionstone/releases/tag/v0.3.1)** · latest | Everything in v0.3.0, plus audio and video stay in sync when a recording's audio ends before its video. |
| **[v0.3.0](https://github.com/ismailarslantas/lionstone/releases/tag/v0.3.0)** | Lionstone Pro & Studio become free and ship in one package, with the new Lionstone Styles. |

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ismailarslantas/lionstone/main/assets/pro-studio/03-smart-editing.jpg" alt="Smart editing: speech-aware cuts, explainable plan, edit via transcript, one-command restore" width="100%">

- **Edits that understand speech** — removes fillers, stutters and false starts, keeps meaningful pauses and never cuts inside a word. Every cut is explained in `plan.json`, editable as text and undoable with one command.
- **Creative Suite** — 133 original looks, effects, transitions and motion presets, 25 animated text styles, karaoke captions and an agent that picks a consistent look for you.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ismailarslantas/lionstone/main/assets/pro-studio/05-pro-workflow.jpg" alt="Pro workflow: multicam, style learning, Shorts and Reels, export to DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut and Premiere" width="100%">

- **Pro workflow** — multicam with automatic active-speaker switching, style learning from videos you like, a Shorts/Reels generator with face-tracked 9:16 reframing, and export to DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro and Premiere Pro.
- **Team tools (formerly Studio)** — roles, brand kits, locked team presets with history, and one look applied to a whole season.
- **Lionstone Styles** — style codes like `O6 W3 A3 G5 S4 P3` that turn into a real edit command.
- **QA before delivery** — checks sync, black and frozen frames, loudness, clicks at every join, caption timing and text covering faces before it says "done".
- **Works with Claude** — a built-in MCP server lets you edit a video by talking to your assistant.

```bash
pip install "lionstone[all] @ git+https://github.com/ismailarslantas/lionstone"
lionstone doctor
```
